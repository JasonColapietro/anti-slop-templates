Common questions

Things people
actually ask.

What exactly gets registered on-chain?
A SHA-256 hash of your file, your author identity, a timestamp, and your chosen license terms. Suede never stores the raw file — only the cryptographic fingerprint that proves it existed, authored by you, at that moment.
Do my records disappear if I cancel?
No. On-chain records are permanent. Cancelling Studio drops new work to Creator limits; everything already registered stays on the registry forever.
What counts as a "registration"?
Each unique file hash submitted to the registry. Re-registering the same file (e.g., to update license terms) uses another slot but creates a versioned link to the original.
How does annual billing work?
Annual Studio is billed as $158 — 10 months at the monthly rate, two months free. Switch periods from your account settings at any renewal date.
What are "agent-readable rights"?
When an AI agent or licensing platform queries your work, it reads your ERC-8004 record directly — no middleman. Studio and Label plans expose this interface so deals close while you're focused on making things.
Can I get a custom contract for my label?
Yes — that's the Label plan. Email hello@suedeai.ai and we'll tailor a deal to your catalog size, chain preferences, and finance requirements.
