Common questions

The things
worth asking.

Can I really use Folio for free, forever?
Yes. Reader is a permanent free tier, not a trial. You get the full typographic editor and one active project with no expiry. We'd rather earn an upgrade than extract one.
What happens to my writing if I cancel?
Nothing bad. Every draft exports as plain text — a format that will outlive every subscription service on earth. Your local archive stays intact. You leave with everything you came with.
How does annual billing actually work?
You pay for ten months and get twelve. Writer drops from $8 to $6.67/month; Studio from $20 to $16.67/seat. We collect the annual total up front and don't touch your card again for a year.
Is Folio really local-first? Where does my data live?
Your drafts are files on your disk first. Cloud sync (Writer and Studio) mirrors them to our servers for cross-device access, but you never need the internet to open, write, or export. We are not the custodian of your work — you are.
Does Folio use my writing to train AI?
No. We have no language model in the editor and no interest in one. Your prose is yours. It is not a training set, a product signal, or a dataset. This will not change with a policy update buried in an email.
Can Studio scale beyond 25 seats?
Contact us. We work directly with presses, journals, and writing programs that need custom seat counts, billing terms, and onboarding. Volume pricing is available and the conversation is free.
